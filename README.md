## 4. Chapter learning format — 15 steps

हर syllabus unit को learning chapter में बदलते समय यह क्रम अपनाएँ:

1. Chapter Introduction and Learning Objectives
2. Complete Syllabus Mapping
3. Basic Concepts
4. Topic-wise Explanation — सरल हिंदी
5. Scientific Terminology — English + हिंदी अर्थ
6. Important Definitions
7. Diagrams and Labelling
8. Examples and Applications
9. Comparison and Differences
10. Short and Long Questions
11. MCQ Practice with answers and explanations
12. Active Recall
13. Revision Summary and Test
14. Weak Topics Recovery
15. Final Chapter Examination

इसके साथ जहाँ उपयोगी हों: colourful notes, labelled diagrams, mind map, YouTube lecture links, print-friendly PDF, question bank और revision sheets।

## 5. App content rules

- Subject IDs: `biochemistry`, `botany`, `zoology`.
